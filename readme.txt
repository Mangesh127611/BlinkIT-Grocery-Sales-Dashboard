# 🛒 BlinkIT Grocery Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze BlinkIT grocery sales data. The dashboard provides key business insights into sales performance, outlet analysis, product categories, customer ratings, and outlet characteristics through interactive visualizations.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate data visualization, business intelligence, and dashboard development skills using Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard helps answer important business questions such as:

- What is the total sales generated?
- Which outlet type contributes the highest sales?
- Which product categories perform the best?
- How does outlet size affect sales?
- Which outlet location generates maximum revenue?
- What is the sales trend based on outlet establishment year?

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Supermarket Type1 generates the highest sales among all outlet types.
- Tier 3 outlets contribute the largest share of total sales.
- Medium-sized outlets generate the highest revenue.
- Snack Foods is the top-performing product category.
- Sales peaked for outlets established in 2018.
- Average customer rating is **3.92**, indicating generally positive customer satisfaction.
